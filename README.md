# Weed Market Incremental – Full Design Document

---

# Core Vision

A three-phase incremental game that evolves from stable production growth into strategic market manipulation, and finally into high-risk leveraged financial chaos.

The same three weeds remain throughout the entire game. The systems layered on top of them evolve dramatically.

The emotional arc:

* Comfort → Strategy → Power → Greed → Panic → Collapse → Survival → Comeback

---

# STEP 1 – Production Economy (Early Game)

## Purpose

Introduce core mechanics and create a stable economic foundation.

## Core Loop

Buy plants → Produce weed → Sell at fixed price → Buy more plants

## Characteristics

* Predictable growth
* No risk
* Stable exponential scaling
* Clear cause and effect

## Design Principles

* Unlimited plants (no hard cap)
* Exponential cost scaling
* Fixed sell price
* No volatility

## Role in Late Game

Step 1 never disappears.
It becomes a stable background income source.
It acts as:

* A psychological safety net
* Bankruptcy recovery fuel
* Collateral value

Plants are always useful, but eventually inefficient compared to market systems.

---

# STEP 2 – Market Economy (Midgame)

## Purpose

Shift the game from passive scaling to strategic decision-making.

## Core Shift

Weed is no longer simply sold.
It becomes inventory used in a dynamic market.

## Market Features

* Floating price per weed type
* Demand and volatility systems
* Momentum/trend behavior
* Upgradeable price ceilings
* Transaction fees and slippage
* Market manipulation mechanics:

  * **Trading Bots**:

    * Purchasable units (multiple allowed).
    * Can be toggled ON for a limited active duration.
    * After use, enter cooldown.
    * Primary function: actively manipulate price (increase momentum, volatility, or short-term trend bias).
    * Higher tiers unlock stronger price impact, longer runtime, shorter cooldown, or multi-weed influence.
  * **Circular Economy (Artificial Virality Loop)**:

    * Hire shady dealers to sell your weed slightly above market price.
    * You buy back the circulating supply.
    * Each loop causes losses from slippage, fees, and contractor payment.
    * Each loop increases a hidden "virality chance".
    * When virality triggers, demand spikes dramatically, enabling large profit opportunities.
    * Risk: excessive looping increases volatility and potential crash probability.

## Player Tools

* Pump/dump actions
* Virality boosts
* High-frequency trading bots
* Liquidity upgrades
* Ceiling upgrades

## Income Structure

* Production generates inventory
* Market timing generates profit
* Trading can fully replace production as main income
* Production supplements trading

Players can make significantly more money than in Step 1.
Growth becomes spiky instead of smooth.

## Safe Asset System

Introduce long-term appreciating assets / investments:

* Gold
* Real estate
* Bonds or similar instruments

Properties:

* Always trend upward
* Low volatility
* Slower growth
* Used as collateral
* Can be liquidated in emergencies

Safe assets create:

* Strategic tradeoffs
* Stability vs growth decisions
* Bankruptcy survival tools

---

# STEP 3 – Leverage & Financial Chaos (Endgame)

## Purpose

Introduce risk, instability, and extreme wealth swings.

## New Mechanics

* Leverage trading
* Cross margin
* Loose maintenance requirements
* Negative balance allowed
* Margin calls

## Wealth Scaling

Players can:

* Out-earn Step 1 by thousands
* Multiply gains massively through leverage
* Experience rapid booms and catastrophic crashes

## Risk System

Net worth can go negative.

When net worth < 0:

### Margin Call State

* Countdown timer begins
* No new leveraged positions allowed
* Must liquidate assets
* Can sell:

  * Gold
  * Properties
  * Plants (base price)
  * Weed (market only, no leverage)

If debt not resolved before timer ends:
Game Over.

If player recovers:

* Survives
* Keeps Step 2 & 3 unlocked
* Capital heavily reduced
* Must rebuild

This creates a soft-prestige-through-failure system.

---

# Bankruptcy & Recovery Loop

The game must allow:

1. Extreme wealth
2. Total collapse
3. Recovery

Bankruptcy should feel dangerous but survivable.

Plants remain active during recovery.
Safe assets matter.
Smart diversification improves survival chances.

The player experience during collapse:
Panic → Liquidation → Stabilization → Rebuild → Try Again

---

# System Interaction Philosophy

Step 1 = Stability
Step 2 = Intelligence
Step 3 = Risk

Each layer does not replace the previous one.
It amplifies it.

Production → Inventory → Market → Leverage → Chaos

The same three weeds persist through all stages.
No additional weeds are introduced.
Complexity comes from systems, not content quantity.

---

# Emotional Progression Design

Early Game:
Comfort and control.

Mid Game:
Strategic power and timing mastery.

Late Game:
Volatility, adrenaline, greed, fear.

Crash:
Financial panic.

Recovery:
Triumph and resilience.

---

# Design Rules Summary

* No hard cap on plants.
* Soft diminishing returns instead.
* Market income eventually dominates production.
* Safe assets provide long-term stability.
* Leverage introduces real risk.
* Debt and bankruptcy are possible.
* Recovery is possible but costly.
* The three weeds remain the core identity.

---

# Final Vision

The game evolves from a simple incremental weed production simulator into a financial power fantasy that transitions into a controlled economic collapse simulator.

It becomes a story of:
Growth → Greed → Collapse → Survival → Rebuilding → Pushing Further

All built on three weeds.
